## Regression on the verified corpus (honest disclosure)

`BOE-A-2022-10033` (in the v0.4/v0.5 corpus): the `por comisión` form
silently dropped a whole infringement block — the third sanctioned
party (don Arturo Sotillo Alonso, €15,000). The golden expectation was
deliberately updated (title names three respondents); this is exactly
the class of silent coverage loss the defect registry exists to catch.

## Holdout

16/16 documents parse with blocks + sanctions, zero parse issues —
**holdout PASS**. One grammar gap (`A X, por la comisión…` bare
subject-first) was found BY the holdout and fixed generically (D31).

## Known limitations (declared)

- `rule_resolution`: most 2015–2017 infringements cite Ley 24/1988 —
  `legal_rules.yaml` has no per-version rules for that era →
  `NO_RULE_DEFINED`/`UNRESOLVED` is the honest state, not a defect.
- Successor sanctions carry `declared_subject` links — the respondent
  of record is the successor; the predecessor appears as a declared
  respondent.
- `sanction_like` title markers are approximate at enumeration; the
  121 non-sanction items (circulares, bajas, acuerdos) were enumerated
  but intentionally not fetched.
